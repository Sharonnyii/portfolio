Lighting programming in RMIT Capitol Theatre

Designed and programmed lighting for a live studio production staged at Melbourne's Capitol Theatre. Focused on atmospheric, low-contrast washes and negative space rather than high-impact effects, treating light as the emotional register of the piece rather than decoration.

VJing performance

Responsible for visual design and sound design for an immersive VJ performance exploring the relationship between contemporary digital media and Chinese cultural elements — using TouchDesigner, a sound synthesizer and live instrument playing, with 3D modelling in Maya.

Visual screening on Federation Square

Original work presented at Federation Square, Melbourne. Developed an endless-loop visual journey exploring the principle of paradox, combining 3D modelling and 2.5D animation to create a continuously evolving digital environment. The work explores how opposing ideas can coexist within a single visual experience.

Graphic designer for Chao Feng Chinese Orchestra concert

Responsible for the design of concert booklets, posters, tickets and handouts, developing a cohesive visual identity across the concert's communication and audience materials.
